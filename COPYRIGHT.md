# Copyright and Fair Use Notice

## What this repository contains

Gamebook Engine is an engine for reading gamebooks: it rolls the dice, keeps the Листок
путешественника and applies the rules a book states. **It contains no published book.** Books are
loaded as packs from a folder you provide (`BOOKS_DIR`).

The only book in this repository is the demo, «Тропа Стеклянной долины»
(`examples/books/demo`), written for the engine and free to copy and adapt.

## Book packs

A pack of a published book contains that book's text, rules and illustrations, which belong to
its author and publishers. Such packs are not part of this repository and are not covered by its
license. If you prepare one:

- keep it private (a private repository, or only on your own server);
- do not distribute the book's text, sell access to it, or use it to earn money;
- credit the author and the book wherever the book is shown;
- if you enjoy the adventure, buy the book and support the author.

The engine adds nothing to a book's story: it carries out the rules the book already states.

## Takedown

A deployment can link a feedback form from every book's imprint and footer
(`NUXT_PUBLIC_FEEDBACK_URL`). Operators should remove a book promptly when its rights holders ask.
